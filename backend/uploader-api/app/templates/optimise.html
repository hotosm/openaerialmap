Optimize imagery before upload

Shrink a large drone, aircraft or satellite photo so it uploads and publishes faster. It runs in your browser: nothing is sent until you upload the result.

Before you start
  • Only standard color visual imagery (8-bit RGB or RGBA) should be optimized. Do not run elevation data or multispectral images through this process.
  • Keep a copy of your original image, as only the optimized image (slightly lossy) will be uploaded.
  • You need free disk space of about twice the file's size.

Optimize

Large files can take a long time, and we can't estimate how long. The timer shows how long it has been running. You can use other tabs and apps, but keep this one open.
